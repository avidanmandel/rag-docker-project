ScoutMatch FC — Tactical Formation Guidelines (sanitized demo document)

4-3-3
- Useful when reliable full-backs are available.
- Supports attacking width and structured pressing.
- May expose weak full-back positions if coverage is limited.

3-4-3
- Useful when three center-backs provide cover.
- Supports wider attacking pressure.
- Requires strong wide midfield or wing-back coverage.

5-4-1
- Useful when defensive stability is important.
- Useful when the striker can operate alone.
- Useful when the team must remain compact.

5-2-3
- Useful for compact defensive structure and fast transitions.
- Useful when three attacking outlets remain available.

Do not treat opponent-specific real-world facts as documented ScoutMatch evidence.
