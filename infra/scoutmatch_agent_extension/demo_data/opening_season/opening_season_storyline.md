# ScoutMatch FC — Opening-Season Storyline

This is synthetic fictional demo data for the ScoutMatch AI course project.

## Season context

ScoutMatch FC is preparing for a new football season. The primary user is a **Scout / Recruitment Analyst / Professional Assistant** — not the head coach and not management.

## Current squad

The club has **15 current club players**:

- **11 likely starting players**
- **4 substitutes or rotation players**

## Recruitment context

- Combined recruitment budget cap: **100,000 EUR** for new signings this window
- Known weaknesses: limited right-back depth, limited goalkeeper cover
- The club has already scouted **8 external recruitment candidates**
- External candidates are preloaded scouting reports — they are **not** signed club players

## Analyst workflow

1. Inspect current squad and depth weaknesses
2. React to coach briefs (injury, suspension, unavailability)
3. Compare preloaded recruitment candidates
4. Recommend tactical adjustments to the head coach (read-only)
5. Submit player recommendations to management (confirmation required)
6. Prepare proposed lineups for head-coach review (confirmation required)
7. Generate visual lineup boards (read-only display)

## Status wording

- Recommendation submitted to management → **PENDING_MANAGEMENT_APPROVAL**
- Proposed lineup saved → **PENDING_HEAD_COACH_REVIEW**

Never claim a player was signed or that management or the head coach approved a decision.
